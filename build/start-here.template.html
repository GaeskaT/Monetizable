The short version

Read the manuscript once. Make it yours in the DOCX. Rehearse it aloud with the outline. Preach it. The slides and guide support the sermon; they are not the sermon.

What’s in the folder

  • 00 · Start Here — this sheet.
  • 01 · Preaching Manuscript — {{WORD_COUNT}} words in two formats. PDF to read; DOCX to edit.
  • 02 · One-Page Outline — movements, timings, lines to land, and the 50-minute cut.
  • 03 · Small-Group Guide — eight questions and leader notes for a follow-up group.
  • 04 · Slides (PPTX) — twelve 16:9 slides, editable in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides.
  • 05 · Slides (PNG) — the same twelve slides as images for a projector or video.
  • 06 · Preaching License — one page. Keep it with your order records.

Make it yours

  • Work in the DOCX, not the PDF. Change names, local details, and illustrations. Put your own stories in your own voice.
  • Keep the evidence. {{EVIDENCE_NOTE}}
  • Update the title page if you change the title or primary text.
  • The deck is fully editable. All text is live. Georgia sets the headings and Candara the body; if either is missing on your machine, swap both for fonts you have.
  • Read it aloud once before Sunday. If a sentence trips you, change it.

The license at a glance

One license covers one congregation or campus, any preacher on staff. It does not expire.

You may

  • Preach and teach this sermon in one congregation or campus, as often as you like.
  • Edit the manuscript for local context and your own voice.
  • Record and stream it as part of normal services.
  • Print the outline and guide for your preaching team and groups.
  • Keep the files on a private, access-controlled system.

You may not

  • Share the files with preachers outside your congregation.
  • Post the manuscript publicly or sell it.
  • Republish it as a standalone work, edited or not.
  • Use one license across multiple campuses.
  • Use the material at a paid conference or event without written permission.

Your edits are yours; the underlying manuscript remains the property of Silenced Saints. No attribution is required. The one-page license is the full grant; keep it with your order records.
