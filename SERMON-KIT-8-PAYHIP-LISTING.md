## Full description

**A complete sermon for the pulpit — not another study.**

“The Valley Breathes” is a 6,810-word sermon on Ezekiel 37:1–14. The prophet is carried into a valley covered with bones — very many, very dry. God asks, “Son of man, can these bones live?” Ezekiel answers, “O Lord GOD, you know.” Then the word comes, the bones assemble, the wind blows, and a graveyard becomes an army. The sermon follows the death, the question, the word, the breath, and the opened graves all the way to the empty tomb.

It is written to be preached, not merely read. The manuscript has an introduction, five movements, application, an invitation, and a guided response. It keeps the gospel explicit, handles the vision in its exile setting, and refuses to turn resurrection into a pep talk. The slides are editable. The small-group guide lets the sermon keep working after Sunday.

**What’s included**

- **Preaching manuscript** — 6,810 words, about 57 minutes with the full response (55–60 with normal variation). A 22-page PDF to read and an editable DOCX to make your own.
- **One-page outline** — movements, timings, lines to land, guardrails, and a 50-minute cut.
- **12-slide deck** — editable 16:9 PPTX for PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides.
- **12 slide images** — the same slides as 1280×720 PNGs for a projector or video.
- **Small-group guide** — eight questions and leader notes for a follow-up group.
- **One-page preaching license** — permission to preach, edit, record, and stream in one congregation or campus. It does not expire.
- **Start Here sheet** — what is in the folder, how to edit the manuscript, and how to take it to the pulpit.
- **Instant download** — one ZIP file with everything above.

**The sermon, not the study**

The Forgotten Figures Field Guide is a nine-session study for groups. This is one finished sermon for the pulpit. It stands on its own; you do not need the study to preach it.

**Built for the preacher**

- Edit freely in the DOCX. Change names, local details, and illustrations. Put your own stories in your own voice.
- Keep the evidence: the valley of dry bones, the two-stage animation, the ruach wordplay, “I will open your graves,” and the promise of the Spirit are doing theological work, and the cross and empty tomb are the turn.
- Preach it once or return to it years later. The license does not expire.
- Record or stream it as part of your congregation’s normal services.

**Who it is for**

Pastors, church planters, bi-vocational preachers, guest preachers, and teachers who want a faithful, finished draft to make their own. If you have ever looked at a life, a family, a church, or a hope and seen only dry bones, this sermon is for you. It is also a careful text for funerals, grief, and long seasons of waiting — with a pastoral care note included.

**Handling a hard text**

The vision’s setting, the meaning of “son of man” in Ezekiel, the force of “very many” and “very dry,” the people’s lament in 37:11, the two-stage animation, the range of ruach as wind, breath, and Spirit, and the relationship between the return from Babylon and the final resurrection — the sermon names the debated points and says what the text does and does not claim. It keeps the primary referent in the exile while showing the canonical steps to Christ. It also keeps the gospel explicit: the dead do not raise themselves; Jesus entered the grave and walked out alive. The application moves from the text to the heart without ending on moralism.
