## Full description

**A complete sermon for the pulpit — not another study.**

“The Weightless King” is a 6,840-word sermon on Daniel 5:1–31. A king throws a feast with the gold cups of God, praises gods he can touch, and watches a hand write four words on the wall. The room goes silent. The experts fail. An old exile tells the truth for free. By morning, the kingdom is gone.

It is written to be preached, not merely read. The manuscript has an introduction, five movements, application, an invitation, and a guided response. The outline gives you timings and transition lines. The slides are editable. The small-group guide lets the sermon keep working after Sunday.

**What’s included**

- **Preaching manuscript** — 6,840 words, about 57 minutes with the full response (55–60 with normal variation). A 19-page PDF to read and an editable DOCX to make your own.
- **One-page outline** — movements, timings, lines to land, and a 50-minute cut.
- **12-slide deck** — editable 16:9 PPTX for PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides.
- **12 slide images** — the same slides as PNGs for a projector or video.
- **Small-group guide** — eight questions and leader notes for a follow-up group.
- **One-page preaching license** — permission to preach, edit, record, and stream in one congregation or campus. It does not expire.
- **Start Here sheet** — what is in the folder, how to edit the manuscript, and how to take it to the pulpit.
- **Instant download** — one ZIP file with everything above.

**The sermon, not the study**

The Forgotten Figures Field Guide is a nine-session study for groups. This is one finished sermon for the pulpit. It stands on its own; you do not need the study to preach it.

**Built for the preacher**

- Edit freely in the DOCX. Change names, local details, and illustrations. Put your own stories in your own voice.
- Keep the evidence: Belshazzar’s co-regency, the temple vessels, the handwriting, and the fall of Babylon to Cyrus in 539 BC.
- Preach it once or return to it years later. The license does not expire.
- Record or stream it as part of your congregation’s normal services.

**Who it is for**

Pastors, church planters, bi-vocational preachers, guest preachers, and teachers who want a faithful, finished draft to make their own. If you have ever stood in a room where the gold was real and the weight was missing, this sermon is for you.
